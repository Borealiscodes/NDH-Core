# ⭐ **Correct Order (NDH‑CORE Charter v1.2)**

This is the safe, constitutional order.

---

# 🌑 **Why this order is correct**

## 1. **Stability Envelope must come first**  
The Stability Envelope defines:

- CORE altitude boundaries  
- diagnostic containment  
- expressive density limits  
- adjacency suppression rules  

Without it, the lineage audit would have **no altitude frame** to evaluate against.

So the Stability Envelope is the “floor” everything else stands on.

---

## 2. **DX‑Series lineage audit comes second**  
Once the Stability Envelope is defined, you can safely:

- check DX‑1 → DX‑4 continuity  
- verify drift history  
- confirm supersession  
- ensure no adjacency bleed  
- validate diagnostic lane placement  

The lineage audit depends on the Stability Envelope’s altitude rules.

---

## 3. **Altitude Encoding comes last**  
Altitude Encoding v1.0 is the **formal encoding** of:

- CORE altitude markers  
- diagnostic altitude ranges  
- expressive altitude caps  
- membrane altitude boundaries  

It must come **after** the lineage audit, because the audit tells you:

- what altitudes were violated  
- what altitudes need correction  
- what altitudes need encoding  

Altitude Encoding is the “final seal.”
