This specification assigns NDH operators to the **GBS Holonomy Map**, ensuring that:

- every operator invocation is loop‑checked

- constructive holonomy is reinforced  
- neutral holonomy is preserved

This becomes the **operator‑level safety net** for NDH‑CORE, NDH‑TIDS, and NDH‑CONSTELLATION.

## ⭐ 5 — Operator Safety Net Rules  
These are the rules operators must follow:

### **Rule 1 — All operators must pass holonomy evaluation**  
No operator may execute without holonomy clearance.

### **Rule 2 — Harmful holonomy terminates the operator**  
Termination triggers:

- stability envelope check  
- primitive integrity check  
- representation stack reset  

### **Rule 3 — Constructive holonomy reinforces the operator**  
Constructive loops strengthen the math stack.

### **Rule 4 — Neutral holonomy preserves the operator**  
Neutral loops are allowed but logged.

### **Rule 5 — Comparative geometry operators must remain reversible**  
No TIDS operator may elevate into governance.

### **Rule 6 — Accessibility operators must remain cooled**  
AGL envelopes cannot descend into trauma geometry.
