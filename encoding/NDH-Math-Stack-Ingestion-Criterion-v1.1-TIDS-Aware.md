# 🧭 **NDH‑CORE — Criterion for Ingestion of Math Stack Bits (TIDS‑Aware Edition)**  
### *Reference Document • Encoding Clarification • Multi‑Altitude Intake Rules*

---

## ⭐ 0 — Identity & Purpose  
This document defines the **rules NDH uses to ingest mathematical artifacts** across:

- **AGL v2.0** (Accessibility Geometry Layer)  
- **NDH‑TIDS** (Relational Geometry Layer)  
- **NDH‑CORE** (Safeguard Layer)  
- **NDH‑CONSTELLATION** (Formal Math Layer)  
- **Phase‑11** (Stability Envelope)  

It ensures that math entering NDH is:

- altitude‑correct  
- encoding‑compatible  
- relationally stable  
- accessibility‑aligned  
- non‑contradictory  
- reversible  
- non‑activating  

This is the **front‑door filter** for the MkII Math Stack Reconstruction Suite.

---

# ⭐ 1 — Ingestion Criterion Overview  
A math artifact may be ingested **only if it satisfies all seven criteria**:

- **Altitude Clarity**  
- **Encoding Compatibility**  
- **Operator Signature Completeness**  
- **Tensor/Holonomy/Tile Alignment**  
- **Cross‑System Non‑Contradiction**  
- **Accessibility Compliance**  
- **Comparative Geometry Safety** ← *new, TIDS‑aware*

This seventh criterion is added because of the AGL ↔ TIDS comparative grammar you provided.

---

# ⭐ 2 — Criterion 1: Altitude Clarity  
Artifacts must declare their altitude:

- **AGL** — sensory + relational envelopes  
- **TIDS** — relational comparative geometry  
- **CORE** — safeguard + reconstruction  
- **CONSTELLATION** — formal math + machine‑readable operators  
- **Phase‑11** — stability envelope  

Mixed‑altitude artifacts must be split before ingestion.

---

# ⭐ 3 — Criterion 2: Encoding Compatibility  
Artifacts must specify:

- tensor rank notation  
- holonomy loop notation  
- tile encoding grammar  
- adjacency lane notation  
- crossmap binding rules  
- accessibility math primitives  
- comparative geometry grammar (if TIDS‑related)  

Artifacts missing encoding cannot be ingested.

---

# ⭐ 4 — Criterion 3: Operator Signature Completeness  
Artifacts must define:

- operator name  
- operator altitude  
- operator domain  
- operator codomain  
- operator posture (dual/non‑dual)  
- operator safety constraints  
- operator accessibility summary  

Incomplete operators cannot be ingested.

---

# ⭐ 5 — Criterion 4: Tensor/Holonomy/Tile Alignment  
Artifacts must align with NDH’s math primitives:

- **tensor grammar**  
- **holonomy semantics**  
- **tile encoding algebra**  

Artifacts introducing contradictions cannot be ingested.

---

# ⭐ 6 — Criterion 5: Cross‑System Non‑Contradiction  
Artifacts must not contradict:

- AGL envelopes  
- TIDS relational geometry  
- CORE safeguard rules  
- CONSTELLATION math grammar  
- Phase‑11 stability envelope  

Contradictory artifacts are quarantined.

---

# ⭐ 7 — Criterion 6: Accessibility Compliance  
Artifacts must include:

- dual‑layer math primitives  
- metaphor‑safe summaries  
- altitude‑safe explanations  
- non‑numeric symbolic math  
- accessibility posture  

Accessibility is a **math invariant**.

---

# ⭐ 8 — Criterion 7: Comparative Geometry Safety (TIDS‑Aware)  
Artifacts referencing TIDS must:

- use comparative geometry grammar  
- remain reversible  
- remain non‑constitutional  
- remain non‑governance  
- remain altitude‑bounded  
- avoid mixing AGL ↔ TIDS  
- avoid blending relational ↔ accessibility geometry  

This criterion is derived directly from your AGL ↔ TIDS comparative spec.

---

# ⭐ 9 — Ingestion Pipeline  
Artifacts that pass all seven criteria enter the pipeline:

- **Encoding Verification**  
- **Altitude Assignment**  
- **Operator Binding**  
- **Tensor/Holonomy/Tile Integration**  
- **Crossmap Alignment**  
- **Accessibility Embedding**  
- **Comparative Geometry Safety Check**  
- **Safeguard Activation**

Artifacts that fail any stage are quarantined.
