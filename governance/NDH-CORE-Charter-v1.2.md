# 🌑 **NDH‑CORE Charter v1.2**  
### *Governance • Altitude Law • Encoding Compatibility*

>  
> It is the governance seal that allows the raw math to emerge safely.

---

## 1. **Charter Mandate**  
NDH‑CORE Charter v1.2 governs:

- altitude boundaries  
- encoding compatibility  
- containment rules  
- non‑activation constraints  
- CONSTELLATION integration  
- post‑Charter math formalization  

It is the **legal layer** between CORE and CONSTELLATION.

---

## 2. **Altitude Law (v1.2)**  
The Charter defines four altitude classes:

- **ROOTS‑ALT‑0** — expressive origin  
- **PROV‑ALT‑1** — provenance membrane  
- **CORE‑ALT‑2** — diagnostic altitude  
- **CONST‑ALT‑3** — geometric activation altitude  

Rules:

- No downward collapse into ROOTS  
- No upward adjacency into CONSTELLATION  
- No numeric curvature inside CORE  
- No invariant verification inside CORE  

---

## 3. **Encoding Compatibility Clause**  
The Charter binds the three encoding systems:

1. **CORE Semantic Encoding (v1.0)**  
2. **CONSTELLATION Geometric Encoding**  
3. **Governance Encoding (Charter v1.2)**  

Compatibility rules:

- Semantic markers may propagate upward  
- Geometric primitives may not propagate downward  
- Governance encoding mediates all interactions  
- No layer may activate another without Charter approval  

---

## 4. **Containment & Non‑Activation Clause**  
The Charter enforces:

- no membrane binding  
- no adjacency matrices  
- no curvature thresholds  
- no activation primitives  
- no invariant calculus  

inside CORE‑ALT‑2.

CONSTELLATION may host these **only after** Charter v1.2.

---

## 5. **CONSTELLATION Integration Authorization**  
The Charter authorizes:

- geometric placeholder activation  
- altitude slot binding  
- symbolic adjacency lanes  
- pre‑geometric containers  

But **not**:

- curvature  
- adjacency matrices  
- invariant verification  
- altitude calculus  

These remain forbidden until **Charter v1.3**.

---

## 6. **Post‑Charter Math Formalization Clause**  
The Charter v1.2 permits:

- formalization of raw math  
- Lean invariant verification  
- geometric altitude binding  
- membrane geometry activation  

but **only inside CONSTELLATION**, and **only after**:

- CORE Altitude Review  
- CONSTELLATION Integration v1.0  
- Charter v1.2 ratification  

This is the constitutional moment when math becomes safe.
