# NDH-CORE Governance Directive: SVG→PNG Embodiment Pipeline

## 1. Purpose

This directive defines the canonical SVG→PNG embodiment pipeline for NDH-governed
systems. It operationalizes the Constellation Rendering Progression Invariant by
converting invariant geometry (SVG) into accessibility-first surfaces (PNG) without
violating dignity, stability, or holonomy.

The pipeline is a **governance-bound mechanism**, not a free-form rendering
strategy.

---

## 2. Governance Constraints

All SVG→PNG embodiment must respect:

- **Shape-first revelation:** SVG encodes invariant geometry; it cannot be skipped.
- **Accessibility-first embodiment:** PNG is the primary visual surface.
- **Dignity-first rendering:** No harmful, overwhelming, or coercive gradients.
- **Holonomy-stable traversal:** Embodiment must preserve return paths and stability.
- **Triad alignment:** Ethics (TISD), posture (Zen), accessibility (NDH-PLATFORMS).

No subsystem may introduce a pipeline that bypasses these constraints.

---

## 3. Canonical Pipeline Stages

### 3.1 SVG (Invariant Geometry)

- Input: SVG representing constellation geometry, stability envelopes, or rights
  manifolds.
- Properties:
  - Encodes curvature and holonomy.
  - Is governance-grade, not user-facing.
  - Must be produced under ethical unlocking and posture interpretation.

### 3.2 Curvature Filtering

- Apply governance-approved filters:
  - Remove non-essential decorative elements.
  - Preserve stability envelopes and critical curvature.
  - Ensure no collapse reenactment or trauma replication.
- Output: SVG′ (embodiment-ready geometry).

### 3.3 Accessibility Mapping

- Map SVG′ regions to accessibility semantics:
  - High-stability zones → calm, low-contrast gradients.
  - Risk or collapse zones → clearly marked but non-overwhelming cues.
  - Neutral zones → minimal visual noise.
- This mapping is **rule-based**, not aesthetic-first.

### 3.4 PNG Rendering

- Render PNG from SVG′ + accessibility mapping:
  - Resolution chosen for clarity, not spectacle.
  - Color and contrast tuned for accessibility standards.
  - No flashing, harsh transitions, or coercive emphasis.
- PNG becomes the **primary visual embodiment surface**.

### 3.5 Haptics / Audio (Optional Embodiment Channels)

- Haptics and audio may be layered on top of the PNG:
  - Haptics: gentle, bounded feedback for stability and risk comprehension.
  - Audio: calm, descriptive cues, never alarming or coercive.
- These channels must follow the same accessibility and dignity constraints.

---

## 4. Prohibited Behaviors

The pipeline must **never**:

- Skip SVG (geometry) and render PNG directly from ad hoc logic.
- Use embodiment to shock, overwhelm, or retraumatize.
- Encode collapse reenactment or harm amplification as “experience.”
- Introduce non-governed aesthetic layers that obscure stability information.
- Break holonomy by making traversal non-reversible or non-comprehensible.

Any such behavior is a violation of NDH-CORE governance.

---

## 5. Relationship to NDH-CORE Invariants

This pipeline is subordinate to:

- **Constellation Rendering Progression Invariant**
- **NDH-CORE Triad Summary**
- **NDH-CORE Governance Index**

If any conflict arises, the invariants override the pipeline.

Governance binds the pipeline.**

All NDH systems must treat this pipeline as the canonical embodiment path.
