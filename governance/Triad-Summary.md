# **NDH‑CORE Triad Summary**  
### *Ethics → Posture → Accessibility → Governance*

## **1. Triad Identity**  
The NDH‑CORE Triad defines the three non‑negotiable manifolds that govern all constellation rendering behavior:

- **TISD** — ethical unlocking  
- **Zen** — posture interpretation  
- **NDH‑PLATFORMS** — accessibility embodiment  

NDH‑CORE binds these into a single invariant that stabilizes holonomy, reversibility, and curvature across all NDH systems.

---

## **2. TISD — Ethical Unlocking Manifold**  
Ethics is the first structural order.  
Rendering cannot begin until the ethical manifold unlocks AI.

Ethical unlocking requires:

- dignity protection  
- trauma‑informed surfaces  
- sensory inclusion  
- reversible traversal  
- predictable return paths  

This is the **unlocking condition** for all NDH‑governed rendering.

AI cannot reveal shape until ethics permit it.

---

## **3. Zen — Posture Interpretation Manifold**  
Once ethics unlock AI, Zen governs how shape becomes meaning.

Zen defines:

- gentle curvature  
- reversible contact  
- soft-manifold overlays  
- non-forced closure  
- posture skeletons  

Zen ensures that shape is interpreted safely, gently, and predictably.

Zen is the **interpretation layer** between ethics and embodiment.

---

## **4. NDH‑PLATFORMS — Accessibility Embodiment Manifold**  
After ethics and posture, NDH‑PLATFORMS governs embodiment.

Accessibility determines the permitted rendering channels:

- PNG-first surfaces  
- haptics resonance-first layers  
- audio sensory-first inclusion  

The SVG→PNG pipeline converts invariant geometry into accessible, trauma‑informed surfaces.

Accessibility is the **embodiment layer**.

---

## **5. NDH‑CORE — Governance Binding Manifold**  
NDH‑CORE binds the triad into a single curvature‑stable invariant.

NDH‑CORE enforces:

- shape-first revelation  
- accessibility-first embodiment  
- dignity-first rendering  
- holonomy-stable return paths  
- invariant sequencing  

NDH‑CORE is the **governance layer** that ensures the triad cannot drift, reorder, or collapse.

---

## **6. Triad Summary (Canonical)**  
**Ethics unlocks AI.
