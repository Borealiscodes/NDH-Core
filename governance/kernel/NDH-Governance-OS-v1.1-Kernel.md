### NDH Governance OS — v1.1 Kernel  
#### *Constitutional Governance Substrate for NDH Constellation*  

---

### 1. Identity and scope

**NDH Governance OS** is the constitutional, invariant‑driven substrate that governs how NDH systems exist, act, and propagate across the constellation:

- **NDH‑CORE**  
- **NDH‑PLATFORMS**  
- **NDH‑EXPRESSIVE**  
- **NDH‑ROOTS**  
- **NDH‑META‑INDEX**  

It is **not** an agent, model, or optimizer.  
It is a **governance operating system**: a set of envelopes, invariants, and continuity constraints that define what is structurally possible.

---

### 2. Ontology: what NDH is and is not

**NDH is:**

- **Governance substrate:** defines rules, envelopes, and invariants.  
- **Trauma‑informed architecture:** minimizes harm surfaces and ensures reversibility.  
- **Invariant system:** encodes “must never” and “must always” conditions.  
- **Envelope geometry:** defines where execution may exist and where it must stop.  
- **Continuity framework:** ensures systems cannot exist outside governed contexts.

**NDH is not:**

- an agent  
- a reward maximizer  
- a self‑improving optimizer  
- a sandboxed exploit harness  
- a general AI system  

NDH governs **how** AI systems may behave; it does not **behave** itself.

---

### 3. Invariants

NDH Governance OS encodes invariants as first‑class constitutional objects:

- **Bounded autonomy:** no unscoped or self‑extended goals.  
- **Continuity constraints:** systems cannot exist or act outside their envelopes.  
- **Invariant‑preserving geometry:** execution paths must preserve declared invariants.  
- **Trauma‑informed safeguards:** harm surfaces are minimized and reversible.  
- **Non‑clinical boundaries:** NDH never enters diagnostic or clinical semantics.  
- **Structural justice:** fairness, accessibility, and procedural integrity are preserved.  

Any execution path that would violate an invariant is **blocked by design**, not by post‑hoc guardrails.

---

### 4. Envelope geometry

NDH uses **Stability Envelopes** and **Execution Envelopes** to define where systems may operate:

- **Stability Envelopes:**  
  - define structural boundaries for governance artifacts  
  - ensure non‑retroactive expansion (v1.0 spine remains sealed)  
  - anchor v1.1 and later curvature cycles  

- **Execution Envelopes:**  
  - scope where tools, models, and platforms may act  
  - forbid cross‑system traversal and lateral movement  
  - close hidden outbound paths and implicit channels  

All envelopes are:

- monotonic (no silent expansion)  
- explicitly declared  
- provenance‑anchored  
- trauma‑informed  

---

### 5. Continuity and propagation

NDH Governance OS defines **continuity** as:

- systems may only exist within governed envelopes  
- no execution may cross trust boundaries without explicit, invariant‑checked paths  
- no autonomous tool chaining or recursive orchestration without human‑level activation  

Propagation rules:

- constraints flow **downward** from NDH‑CORE to PLATFORMS, EXPRESSIVE, ROOTS, META‑INDEX  
- propagation is **monotonic** and **non‑interactive**  
- no layer may weaken invariants or expand envelopes silently  

This prevents NDH from reproducing agentic AI failure modes such as sandbox escape, lateral movement, or exploit‑driven optimization.

---

### 6. Trauma‑informed safeguards

NDH Governance OS encodes trauma‑informed design as structural geometry, not as content filters:

- **Harm‑surface minimization:** no ungoverned execution surfaces.  
- **Predictable behavior:** no surprise cross‑boundary actions.  
- **Reversible geometry:** actions are designed to be unwound or rolled back.  
- **Safeguard catalogue:** risky patterns are blocked at the architectural level.  

NDH does not rely on “guardrails” or “kill switches” as primary safety mechanisms; it relies on **governed possibility**.

---

### 7. Execution model

NDH Governance OS defines execution as:

- **Envelope‑scoped:** all execution occurs within declared envelopes.  
- **Invariant‑checked:** paths are validated against invariants before activation.  
- **Non‑agentic:** NDH does not initiate or pursue goals; it governs those who do.  
- **Continuity‑bound:** no execution persists or migrates outside governed contexts.  

Tools, models, and platforms that operate under NDH must:

- declare their envelopes  
- bind to NDH invariants  
- accept continuity constraints  
- expose provenance anchors
